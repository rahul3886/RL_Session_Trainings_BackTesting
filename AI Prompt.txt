CORE PHILOSOPHY — READ THIS BEFORE WRITING A SINGLE LINE OF CODE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The London session (08:00–17:00 UTC) is the institutional playbook.
The New York session (12:00–21:00 UTC) reads that playbook and responds.
NY consistently respects London's structural levels — its highs, lows,
order blocks, fair value gaps, and liquidity pools. NY either continues
the London trend, reverses off London levels, or sweeps London liquidity
before reversing. This relationship is the edge.

Human traders discovered this by watching thousands of charts and
recognising recurring patterns. We identified 8 scenarios:
  S1 Bullish continuation · S2 Bearish continuation
  S3 High breakout retest · S4 Low breakdown retest
  S5 Range expand up      · S6 Range expand down
  S7 High liquidity grab  · S8 Low liquidity grab

Brain 1 must discover these same patterns — and potentially more —
purely by observing 3 years of London→NY price action through
reinforcement learning. We do NOT pre-label scenarios or hard-code
rules. The patterns emerge from the data. Brain 1 learns what a human
analyst learns: "given what London did, what does NY typically do?"

Brain 2 then uses Brain 1's learned understanding as its context and
learns to place institutional-grade trades during the NY session.

The two brains are trained sequentially:
  Step 2 → Train Brain 1 on London→NY observation (RL)
  Step 3 → Freeze Brain 1, train Brain 2 on NY execution (RL)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 2 — TRAIN BRAIN 1 (LONDON SESSION OBSERVER)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Brain 1 is a reinforcement learning agent that observes the London
session candle by candle and learns to build an internal representation
of what the London session means for the coming NY session.

It learns this the same way a human analyst learns: by watching what
London did and then seeing how NY responded — thousands of times.
After training, Brain 1's learned representation (its encoding) becomes
the context handed to Brain 2.

Brain 1 does NOT place trades. It has no position. Its only job is to
observe and encode.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2.1  BRAIN 1 ENVIRONMENT  (src/brain1/environment.py)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CLASS: Brain1LondonEnv(gym.Env)

EPISODE STRUCTURE:
  One episode = one complete trading day
  The episode has two phases:

  OBSERVATION PHASE (08:00–12:15 UTC):
    Agent receives London candles one at a time (15M).
    At each candle, agent takes an "attention action" — a continuous
    vector that represents what it is attending to in the structure.
    No reward is given during this phase.
    Agent is building its understanding of the session.

  EVALUATION PHASE (12:15–21:00 UTC):
    The London window has closed. Agent can no longer observe
    new London candles. But it now observes NY candles as they
    unfold. At each NY candle, the environment checks:
    "did Brain 1's encoding predict what NY is doing?"
    Reward is given based on prediction accuracy.

OBSERVATION SPACE (31 dimensions):
  This is the raw SMC feature vector of the London session
  as seen by Brain 1 at the current candle. Updated each step.
  Dimensions:

  Session state (7):
    session_bias                # +1/0/-1 so far
    session_range_atr           # high-low / ATR so far
    session_body_ratio          # |close-open| / range
    dist_to_session_high        # from current price, ATR-normalised
    dist_to_session_low
    dist_to_session_mid
    time_elapsed_norm           # minutes into London / 255 (0–1)

  Order blocks (6):
    bull_ob_dist                # nearest bull OB / ATR
    bull_ob_strength            # displacement magnitude
    bear_ob_dist
    bear_ob_strength
    ob_at_high_flag             # bear OB near session high
    ob_at_low_flag              # bull OB near session low

  Fair value gaps (5):
    bull_fvg_unfilled           # 1 if exists
    bull_fvg_dist               # / ATR
    bear_fvg_unfilled
    bear_fvg_dist
    fvg_total_size_atr          # total imbalance magnitude

  Liquidity (6):
    equal_highs_present         # engineered buy-side liquidity above
    equal_lows_present          # engineered sell-side liquidity below
    liq_above_density           # count of equal high clusters
    liq_below_density
    pdh_dist                    # prev day high / ATR
    pdl_dist

  Structure (4):
    choch_bull_recent           # CHoCH up in last 5 candles
    choch_bear_recent
    mss_bull_recent
    mss_bear_recent

  Candle anatomy (3):
    current_body_atr
    current_upper_wick_atr
    current_lower_wick_atr

ACTION SPACE (Box, 8 dimensions, continuous [-1, 1]):
  Brain 1's action is a continuous latent encoding vector.
  This is what gets passed to Brain 2 as context.
  Dimension = settings.brain1.encoding_dim = 8 initially.
  The agent learns what to encode — we do not define it.

  Why continuous action? Because we want Brain 1 to output a
  rich, continuous representation of London session state, not
  a discrete category. PPO with continuous action space
  (using DiagGaussianDistribution) handles this natively.

  Think of it as: Brain 1 is answering the question
  "how do I summarise this London session in 8 numbers?"
  The reward teaches it which 8 numbers matter.

TERMINAL STATE:
  Episode ends at 21:00 UTC (NY session close).
  Brain 1 is evaluated on what NY did during 12:15–21:00.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2.2  BRAIN 1 REWARD FUNCTION  (src/brain1/reward.py)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Brain 1 is rewarded based on how accurately its encoding
predicted NY session behaviour. The rewards are given
RETROSPECTIVELY as NY unfolds — Brain 1 sees the consequences
of its encoding in real time.

This is the key insight: Brain 1's reward signal comes from
the NY session, not the London session. This forces it to
learn exactly the information that is predictive of NY.

REWARD COMPONENTS:

1. DIRECTIONAL ACCURACY REWARD (weight 1.0)
   After NY first 2 candles (12:15–12:45 UTC):
   
   ny_direction = +1 if NY is trading above London Mid
               = -1 if NY is trading below London Mid
   
   If Brain 1's encoding action[0] (the primary bias dimension)
   has the same sign as ny_direction:
     reward += 0.5    (Brain 1 correctly read London bias)
   else:
     reward -= 0.3    (Brain 1 misread the setup)

2. LEVEL RESPECT REWARD (weight 0.5)
   During NY session, at each NY candle:

   If price touches London High and Brain 1 encoding suggests
   resistance (action[1] > 0.3) → reward += 0.3
   
   If price touches London Low and Brain 1 encoding suggests
   support (action[2] > 0.3) → reward += 0.3
   
   If price ignores the level (blow-through) and Brain 1 encoded
   it as significant → reward -= 0.2

3. SWEEP DETECTION REWARD (weight 0.8)
   The highest-value thing Brain 1 can learn:

   If NY spikes above London High by > 3pts and reverses:
     This is a liquidity grab (our Scenario 7).
     If Brain 1's encoding had action[3] > 0.5 (sweep-likely flag):
       reward += 1.0    (Brain 1 anticipated the sweep)
     else:
       reward -= 0.5    (Brain 1 missed the most important signal)

   If NY spikes below London Low by > 3pts and reverses:
     This is Scenario 8.
     If Brain 1's encoding had action[4] > 0.5:
       reward += 1.0
     else:
       reward -= 0.5

4. CONTINUATION ACCURACY REWARD (weight 0.6)
   At NY session close (21:00 UTC):

   If london_bias = +1 and NY closed above London Open:
     continuation occurred.
     If Brain 1 encoding action[0] was > 0.3:
       reward += 0.5
   
   If london_bias = -1 and NY closed below London Open:
     continuation occurred.
     If Brain 1 encoding action[0] was < -0.3:
       reward += 0.5

5. RANGING SESSION ACCURACY (weight 0.4)
   If London was ranging (range < 30pts) and NY expanded:
     direction of expansion matters.
     Brain 1 should encode which direction expansion is
     more likely based on liquidity geometry.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE:
  Brain 1 outputs its encoding action once — at 12:15 UTC
  when the London window closes. The action is cached.
  All subsequent rewards during NY are calculated against
  this cached encoding. This gives Brain 1 clear signal:
  "your 12:15 summary of London predicted/failed to predict
  what NY actually did."

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3.2  STOP LOSS AND TAKE PROFIT — STRUCTURAL (no ATR placement)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

STOP LOSS:
  Long SL  = nearest bull OB low  − (sl_ob_buffer_atr × ATR)
  Short SL = nearest bear OB high + (sl_ob_buffer_atr × ATR)

  If no OB within 2.5 ATR: use nearest swing low/high as SL.

  ATR GATE (reject trade if):
    |entry − SL| < 0.3 × ATR   (too tight, wick will hit it)
    |entry − SL| > 3.0 × ATR   (too wide, R:R destroyed)

TAKE PROFIT — structural waterfall:
  TP1 (close 50% of position) = LondonMid
  TP2 (close 30% of position) = opposite London level (LH or LL)
  TP3 (trail 20% remainder)   = PDH or PDL (whichever is in direction)

  Rule: after TP1 is hit, move SL to breakeven immediately.
        The remaining 50% of the position becomes a free trade.

  Hard close: any open position at 20:45 UTC is closed at market.

R-MULTIPLE: normalise by actual structural SL distance.
  r_multiple = pnl / abs(entry_price - sl_price)
  NOT: pnl / (ATR × constant)
